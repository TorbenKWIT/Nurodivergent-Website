We're so glad you're here. The Neurodiversity Club is a space for ADHD, autistic, dyslexic, and otherwise neurodivergent students (and allies) to connect, support each other, and build a more understanding campus community.

## What to expect

- Low-pressure weekly meetups — come as you are, leave when you need to
- A resource library for accommodations and campus support services
- Events built around sensory-friendly, predictable formats

More posts will follow as we plan our first events. If you have ideas or want to get involved, visit the [Join Us](/join) page.
